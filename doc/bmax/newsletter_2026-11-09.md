# Bmax newsletter — November 9, 2026

**Subject:** A clearer UV workflow in Bmax

**Preview:** Better selection visibility and UV pins across selection modes.

Hi everyone!

A small UV editing update is coming to Bmax, with a few improvements to make everyday unwrapping smoother.

**Keep your selection in sight**
Shell Outline now sits beneath UV edges, vertices and pin markers in Edit Mode, so the white outline no longer covers the details you need to see. This includes selected and unselected vertices.

**See your pins in Edge and Face Mode**
Pinned UV vertices stay visible when you switch selection modes, making it easier to keep track of your unwrap anchors.

**Pin without switching modes**
Use Pin, Unpin or Invert Pins directly in Edge or Face Mode. Selected edges act on their endpoints, and selected faces act on their corners.

The update has been verified on both OpenGL and Vulkan, alongside 243 functional checks.

These improvements are available in the Bmax source and verified local build. The public Windows download has not yet been replaced; they will be included in the next binary update.

Thanks for using Bmax and sharing your feedback!

Happy unwrapping!
